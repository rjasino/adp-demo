**Topic Slug:** chicken-adobo-quick-reel

**Language:** Taglish  
**Audience:** Home cooks 25–45, social media users, food enthusiasts  

---

## 📋 Source / Verified Facts (for reference, not spoken)

1. Authentic flavor using vinegar, soy sauce, garlic, bay leaves, peppercorns  
2. Ratio guideline: ½ cup vinegar : ¼ cup soy sauce  
3. Quick method: marinate → brown → simmer (~20 min) → caramelize with vinegar → finish  
4. Plating shot with steaming rice for the “comfort food” payoff  
5. Optional additions: bay leaves, peppercorns, sugar for caramelization
