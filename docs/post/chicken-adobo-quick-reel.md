**Quick steps (in 30‑45 seconds):**  
1. **Marinate** chicken with vinegar, soy sauce, garlic, peppercorns, and a bay leaf (15 min).  
2. **Brown** the chicken in a pot, then add the rest of the marinade.  
3. **Simmer** for ~20 minutes until tender.  
4. **Caramelize** with a splash of vinegar and a pinch of sugar for that classic finish.  
5. **Serve hot** with steaming rice — the ultimate comfort food. 🍚
