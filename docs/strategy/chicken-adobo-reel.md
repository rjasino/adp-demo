# Content Strategy: Chicken Adobo Reel

## Brief
- Topic: How to cook chicken adobo
- Goal: Educate and inspire home cooks to make authentic Filipino chicken adobo quickly on Instagram/TikTok Reels
- Target audience: Home cooks aged 25-45 who want quick, authentic recipes; social media users who scroll for quick entertainment; food enthusiasts who appreciate authentic Filipino cuisine; busy professionals looking for quick, flavorful weeknight meals
- Geography/platform: Instagram/TikTok Reels (Philippines and diaspora)
- Constraints: 30-45 seconds; vertical 9:16 aspect ratio; must use pantry staples; quick cooking time (under 30 minutes)

## Audience and goal
The target audience consists of home cooks and food enthusiasts seeking authentic Filipino recipes that are quick and doable for weeknight meals. The goal is to showcase that real Filipino chicken adobo can be made in under 30 minutes using pantry staples, inspiring viewers to try the recipe and engage with Filipino cuisine content. The content should feel approachable, authentic, and efficient.

## Research and verified context
- Chicken adobo is considered the national dish of the Philippines, known for its vinegar-soy sauce-garlic base.
- Traditional adobo simmers for 30-45 minutes, but a quick-reel version can achieve flavor in under 30 minutes using higher heat and proper technique.
- Key flavor elements: vinegar, soy sauce, garlic, bay leaves, peppercorns.
- Quick-cook method: Browning chicken first, then simmering with aromatics reduces time while maintaining flavor depth.
- Source: General culinary knowledge; no specific primary source verified.

## Recommended content format
**Step-by-step quick-reel (30-45 seconds)** - This format is recommended because:
- It fits the platform's attention span (15-60 second typical Reel length).
- It clearly demonstrates the cooking process in a logical sequence.
- It allows for key flavor tips and timing within the time limit.
- It performs well on both Instagram and TikTok for tutorial content.
- *Pending user approval: if the user prefers a story-time or quick-tip format instead, that should be confirmed.*

## Strategic direction
- **Content angle/hook:** "Real Filipino chicken adobo in under 30 minutes - no fancy ingredients, just pantry staples."
- **Key messages:**
  1. Authentic flavor using just vinegar, soy sauce, garlic, bay leaves, and peppercorns
  2. Quick and doable for weeknight cooking (under 30 minutes)
  3. Crispy chicken + tangy sauce = comfort food
- **Content beats/proof points:**
  - Quick visual of sizzling chicken in first 3 seconds
  - Shot-by-shot breakdown: marinate → brown → simmer → caramelize with vinegar → finish
  - Emphasize the ratio: 1/2 cup vinegar to 1/4 cup soy sauce for authentic taste
  - Finish with plating shot alongside steaming rice
- **Working title/topic slug:** `chicken-adobo-quick-reel`
