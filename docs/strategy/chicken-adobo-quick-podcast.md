# Content Strategy: Traditional Filipino Dishes Podcast

## Brief
- Topic: Traditional Filipino dishes podcast
- Goal: Educate and inspire listeners about authentic Filipino cuisine through a solo-monologue podcast format
- Target audience: Filipino diaspora (US, Canada, Australia, Europe) ages 25-45; home cooks and foodies seeking cultural connection; non-Filipino food enthusiasts interested in Southeast Asian flavors
- Geography/platform: Podcast audio (available on Spotify, Apple Podcasts, etc.)
- Constraints: Weekly release schedule; single dish per episode; ~12 minutes per episode; Taglish language (Tagalog-English mix)

## Audience and goal
The target audience consists of Filipino diaspora and food enthusiasts seeking authentic Filipino recipes and cultural stories. The goal is to showcase traditional Filipino dishes, their histories, and cooking techniques, inspiring listeners to explore or reconnect with Filipino cuisine. The content should feel approachable, authentic, and efficient for busy listeners.

## Research and verified context
- Filipino cuisine is characterized by flavor profiles: salty (soy sauce), sour (vinegar/tamarind), and savory (garlic, onions)
- Traditional cooking methods: braising (adobo), sour soup (sinigang), roasting (lechon), peanut-based stews (kare-kare)
- Rice is the staple accompaniment to most Filipino meals
- Many traditional dishes have regional variations across the Philippine archipelago
- Podcast format is ideal for storytelling and detailed cultural context

## Recommended content format
**Solo-monologue podcast (~12 minutes per episode, weekly release)**
- Each episode focuses on one traditional Filipino dish
- Structure: Intro (1:30) → Story/Background (3:30) → Recipe Highlight (4:00) → Key Tips (2:00) → Outro (1:00)
- Taglish language throughout for authenticity and broader appeal
- Episode show notes include: quick recipe summary, key tips, listener question prompt

## Strategic direction
- **Content angle/hook per episode:** Each episode highlights the cultural significance, family stories, or regional variations of the featured dish
- **Key messages:**
  1. Every Filipino family has a unique version of classic dishes
  2. Traditional recipes use accessible pantry staples
  3. Cooking is an act of cultural preservation and care
- **Content beats/proof points per episode:**
  - Origin story or regional variation
  - Key ingredients and their significance
  - One standout technique or tip
  - Listener engagement prompt (recipe attempt, family story share)
